# Model card: wafer-map pattern classifier

Generated by `training/wafermap/wm/bundle.py` from the JSON reports at commit `58516138c00c364b783ae7936d65713b533904d9`. Bundle `artifacts/wafermap/v1/`, deployed model `lot-seed1`.

## Intended use

Classify the defect pattern of a wafer map (Center, Donut, Edge-Loc, Edge-Ring, Loc, Near-full, Random, Scratch, none) to help yield engineers triage wafers and lots, say "unknown pattern" when a map does not look like the training data, and find similar past wafers. It recommends; an engineer decides. Patterns are statistically associated with process problems; the model does not identify causes.

## Data

WM-811K (Wu et al., 2015): 811,457 real wafer maps from 46,293 lots, 172,950 labelled. Only labelled maps are used for supervised training. Details: `training/wafermap/reports/wm811k_facts.md`.

Two splits, both with no lot in more than one part (wafers of a lot are correlated):

| Split | Train | Validation | Test | Test lots |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Official (shipped with WM-811K; validation carved from its training part by lot) | 46,190 | 8,165 | 118,595 | 4,953 |
| Lot-grouped (70/15/15 of lots, stratified) | 121,049 | 25,947 | 25,954 | 1,608 |

## Method

- Input: nearest-neighbour resize to 64x64 (no value is invented between no die, pass and fail), one-hot channels `no_die`, `pass`, `fail`.
- Model: compact residual CNN (4 stages of 2 residual blocks, 32-256 channels, 2.83 M parameters); 128-d penultimate embedding.
- Training: focal loss (gamma 2) with class-balanced weights (beta 0.9999), augmentation by the 8 rotations and flips of the square only, AdamW (lr 2e-3, weight decay 0.05), 1 warm-up epoch then cosine decay, bf16 mixed precision, at most 40 epochs, early stopping on validation macro-F1 (patience 8), 3 seeds. No other hyperparameter search. Test sets were used once, after training.
- Calibration: temperature scaling fitted on validation.
- Unknown patterns: Mahalanobis distance on L2-normalised embeddings (shared Ledoit-Wolf covariance), threshold at 95% in-distribution recall on validation; the energy score is kept as a cross-check. Energy was the planned method; it was replaced after the leave-one-class-out results below (0.545 against 0.922), so the choice used those results.
- Confidence intervals: 95%, lot-cluster bootstrap (2,000 resamples of test lots unless stated).

**Finding.** The energy score barely separates unseen classes (mean AUROC 0.545; below 0.5 for Edge-Ring and Near-full, whose unseen wafers get more confident logits than in-distribution ones). Mahalanobis distance on the embeddings does (mean AUROC 0.922). Energy remains in this bundle because `ood.json` v1 fixes it as the method; Mahalanobis parameters ship in `ood_mahalanobis.npz` and should be the primary unknown score. This recommendation is itself informed by the leave-one-class-out results, so those AUROCs are not an independent estimate for the chosen method.

### Lot excursions

The 1,608 lot-grouped test lots (36,749 wafers, labelled or not, patterns predicted by the deployed model) were tested for each pattern against the rate in the validation lots, one-sided binomial with Benjamini-Hochberg at q = 0.05: 379 lots flagged. Check against the same test on true labels (labelled wafers only): 0.65 of the model's defect flags are also label flags, and the model finds 0.78 of the label flags.

Lots with no labelled wafer (35,531 lots) are not tested: the model calls 0.305 of their wafers unknown versus 0.097 in the validation lots, a shift from the labelled population that would dominate any flag.

## Limitations

- The official test set is 93% `none`, the lot-grouped one about 85%; macro-F1 weighs every class equally, so rare classes (Near-full: 23 test wafers) have wide intervals.
- The official split gives a much lower macro-F1 than the lot-grouped one (0.604 ± 0.043 vs 0.900 ± 0.006). Its test lots are the higher-numbered ones and differ from its training lots: models that score well on validation lots drawn from official training call many test `none` wafers Edge-Loc, Center or Loc. One official seed stopped early (best validation epoch 15, 4, 22 for seeds 0, 1, 2) and is reported as measured; seeds were not re-run after the test results were seen.
- Labels were assigned by the dataset's annotators; label noise is unmeasured.
- WM-811K has no timestamps, so "reference period" for the lot tests means the validation lots, not an earlier time window. The binomial test treats wafers of a lot as independent under the null; a lot-level process problem is exactly what it is meant to detect.
- The unknown-pattern test can only use the 8 known defect classes as stand-ins for new patterns; real novel patterns may be easier or harder to flag.
- Maps are resized to 64x64; very large maps (up to 300x205 dies) lose detail.
- Lot predictions for unlabelled wafers are model outputs, not ground truth.
